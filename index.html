About

I am a second-year M.Eng. student in Computer Technology at the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Chunhua Shen. Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University in 2025, as a member of Chu Kochen Honors College and the Turing Honors Class.

My research interests lie in multimodal, efficient inference and 3D vision. Feel free to reach out via email for discussion or collaboration.
